Why Parcelytics

Property tax data shouldn't require detective work.

County appraisal districts and tax offices already keep the definitive record on every parcel — appraised value, exemptions, ownership, tax rates, what was billed and what was paid. It's public, it's official, and it always has been. That was never the problem.

The problem is where it lives. A property's full tax picture is usually split across an appraisal district's site, a separate tax office's site, and whatever else happens to still be online — three systems, none built to talk to each other. The information isn't unreliable — it's just spread across sources that were never built to connect.

Parcelytics connects directly to each county's own appraisal district and tax office and brings that record together at the parcel level. One page, one property, its entire tax history in one place — sourced, labeled, and traceable back to where it came from.
